    Gram Panchayat Portal

    A prototype public-service web application designed to bridge the trust gap between village residents and local administration through open, anonymous complaint reporting and verifiable resolution tracking.

    ⚠️ Prototype Notice: This website is an educational demonstration. It is not an official government portal and does not dispatch real-world municipal workers.

    Privacy & Anonymity

    • Zero Forced Sign-in: Citizens can report grievances without supplying phone numbers, email addresses, or Aadhaar details.
    • Anti-Retaliation Protection: Submitter IP and personal device identifiers are not displayed on any public dashboard.
    • Open Data Safety: Only civic issue details, ward location, and photo evidence are visible on the village ledger.

    Accessibility & Inclusivity

    • High-Contrast Ready: High contrast color mode available for visually impaired users.
    • Scalable Typography: Instant text magnification buttons located at the top bar.
    • Bilingual Vision: Built with semantic HTML to support future localized Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) screen readers.
    • Low Bandwidth Optimized: Lightweight CSS & vanilla JS without bloated external dependencies.
