Simple 4-Step Process

How It Works

Designed for ease of use by any villager. No complicated paperwork, no visits to government offices.

Step 1

Submit a Complaint

Select your village ward, describe the issue, and optionally snap and attach a photo. You can choose to remain 100% anonymous without sharing your name or contact number.

Step 2

Receive a Reference Number

As soon as your complaint is submitted, the system produces a unique tracking token (e.g., GP-2026-W03-014). Keep this code safe to monitor updates anytime.

Step 3

Track the Progress

Type your reference ID into the tracking portal. View every milestone as your grievance moves from Submitted to Inspection Scheduled and Work in Progress.

Step 4

View Action Taken

Before an issue is closed, the Gram Panchayat team must log the resolution details, contractor/work notes, and verification photos visible to all village members.

🛡️ Small-Group Privacy Protection: Wards with fewer than 2 complaints are automatically aggregated into "Other Village Wards" to prevent deanonymizing individual residents in sparsely populated areas.

1. Public Grievance Ledger

Every submitted issue (with personal identities stripped for privacy) is logged to a publicly browsable ward ledger. Residents can verify if a neighbor has already reported the same broken pipeline, avoiding duplicate complaints and saving Panchayat resources.

Open Access

2. Timestamped Audit Trails

Complaints cannot be silently deleted or ignored. Every change in status—from inspection assignment to work order approval—records the exact date, time, and designated ward officer note into an immutable history trail.

Audit Integrity

3. Photo-Verified Resolution

An issue cannot be marked as "Resolved" with a simple checkbox. Gram Panchayat field staff must upload a verifiable completion photo and work summary, enabling villagers to confirm whether actual repair work took place.

Verified Resolution

4. Citizen Feedback & Re-Open

If a reported issue is prematurely marked resolved or poorly executed, residents of that ward can flag the ticket for re-inspection within 7 days, maintaining continuous democratic oversight.

Community Oversight

Privacy & Anonymity

  • Zero Forced Sign-in: Citizens can report grievances without supplying phone numbers, email addresses, or Aadhaar details.
  • Anti-Retaliation Protection: Submitter IP and personal device identifiers are not displayed on any public dashboard.
  • Open Data Safety: Only civic issue details, ward location, and photo evidence are visible on the village ledger.

Accessibility & Inclusivity

  • High-Contrast Ready: High contrast color mode available for visually impaired users.
  • Scalable Typography: Instant text magnification buttons located at the top bar.
  • Bilingual Vision: Built with semantic HTML to support future localized Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) screen readers and text.
  • Low Bandwidth Optimized: Lightweight CSS & vanilla JS without bloated external frameworks.
